    Weather

    Weather data from Open-Meteo. Pressure is sea-level mb, shown from 6am to 9pm each day. The score weighs pressure trend most, then wind, sky, temperature and moon phase, with a small penalty for very high or very low pressure. Suggested times compare three-hour windows around dawn and dusk. The downwind bank is only a rule of thumb; lake shape and accessible swims matter.
